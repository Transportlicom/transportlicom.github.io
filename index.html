A shipping marketplace built on real prices

Transportli is a marketplace for shipping cars and freight. You post one request, independent vetted carriers send bids, and you compare real quotes before you pick one.

Behind the marketplace is our own price data: more than 1,600 US car shipping lanes, hundreds of real bills of lading, and carrier quotes for LTL freight. We publish what we learn as free calculators, weekly price indexes, studies and a public API, so you can check any quote against the market before you book.

Transportli is a marketplace, not a carrier. The carrier you choose performs the shipment.
